# Journal

Yours to write, in your own words. Ten minutes at each weekly close. These notes turn into the README's design-decisions section, a blog post, and real answers to "what was the hardest bug?" and "what would you do differently?".

Write it yourself. Notes an AI wrote for you are useless in an interview because you won't remember them.

## Weekly notes

Copy this block for each week.

```
## Week N — YYYY-MM-DD
- Built:
- Hardest part and how I got through it:
- Something I understand now that I didn't a week ago:
- A decision I'd explain differently now:
- LLM spend this week:
- Behind / on track / ahead, and what I'm doing about it:
```

## Hardest bugs

Log these when they happen, not later. Symptom → what I thought it was → what it actually was → how I found it.

## What I'd do differently

Add a line whenever you catch yourself thinking it.

## Interview answers to rehearse (from spec.md §10)

Fill each with 3–4 bullet points from what actually happened.

1. How do you know an LLM judge is trustworthy?
2. Why not just use an existing tool?
3. How would this scale to a large customer?
4. A customer says their agent got worse last week. Walk me through it.
5. What was the hardest bug?
6. What would you do differently?
